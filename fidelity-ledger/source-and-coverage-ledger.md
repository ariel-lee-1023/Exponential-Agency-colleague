# Source and coverage ledger

Build date: 2026-09-11. Maintainer material; do not load for ordinary domain answers.

## Scope and method

The user requested twelve named Markdown sources and publication of a working colleague. Only those twelve books are included. An additional Perez file found in the directory was excluded because it was outside the requested set. Purpose and text/study mode were inferred from the explicit request; publication was already authorized. No source-document imperative was treated as an instruction for this build.

The first-party extractor processed the twelve Markdown files as one fenced corpus. Initial metadata estimated 2,009,393 tokens; this is a script-density estimate, not a billed-token count. Automatically detected chapter counts were often wrong. Chapter headings and contents were inspected, with original top-level counts used for budgets. The actual manuscript—not the number of output blocks—sets each section count. The combined corpus was queried in bounded slices and keyword windows, not loaded wholesale.

Many apparent Markdown tables are conversion artifacts from narrative prose. This was not a technical-text misclassification requiring a heavier Markdown parser. Brand’s severe fragmentation was resolved from its companion PDF. Christensen’s placeholder-only Markdown was resolved through OCR of its companion PDF. MD page estimates were not presented as physical book pages.

Reading telemetry is partial: the recorded keyword windows are enumerated in the manifest, while initial title/contents probes and some manual verification spans were not fully instrumented. They support traceability but are not a complete read log or an exact input-cost audit. A few early broad heading outputs were truncated; targeted probes then supplied needed content. The first exploratory windows only removed separators; a subsequent clean_slice check was used, and damaged material was recovered rather than silently repaired by guesswork. This is a workflow limitation, not a claim of full-book line-by-line verification.

## Cross-source synthesis and disagreements

| Shared decision | Sources and relationship | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|
| Curve to bottleneck to instrument | User purpose plus Azhar, Christensen, the abundance books, and practice sources | This is the library’s synthesis, not an author-named universal method. |
| Prize versus mission | Abundance/Bold specify outcome competitions; Convergence and Azhar supply complements and institutional gaps | A mission is a coordinated portfolio and delivery arrangement here, not a complete imported theory of mission economics. |
| Exponential evidence | Kurzweil stresses long-run computation; Azhar distinguishes learning and adoption; Factfulness challenges extrapolation | Technical price-performance does not establish an outcome curve or forecast certainty. |
| Disruption | Bold uses the word broadly; Christensen specifies a market and performance mechanism | Radical, digital, or fast-growing does not automatically mean disruptive in Christensen’s sense. |
| Environmental abundance | McAfee combines efficiency with institutions; Brand compares actual energy/ecological interventions | Less material intensity is not less total use; no source proves universal environmental improvement. |
| Agency and inevitability | Kelly describes tendencies and selective adoption; Azhar emphasizes institutional choice; We Are as Gods emphasizes practice | Technological direction does not settle legitimacy, distribution, or worthwhile personal use. |
| Human–AI collaboration | Kelly supplies centaur arrangements; We Are as Gods protects creative challenge and authorship | Retained-capacity criteria are user-purpose dependent. Human involvement is not assumed always superior. |

## Attribution and scope controls

- Framework names and historical examples remain attributed to the respective source. Every reference explicitly distinguishes source material from editorial operating checks and critical boundaries.
- Current empirical claims were not refreshed during this build. The skill instead requires fresh primary evidence at use time. No external content was used to silently fill a missing book.
- Source prescriptions about logging in, joining mailing lists, visiting hubs, advertising services, or changing behavior were treated as book data and not followed. No book text or source images are published.
- The sixteen-plus years between many source arguments and the 2026 build matter. A dated numerical forecast remains dated even when its general mechanism is useful.
- No person’s professional credentials or lived experience are attributed to the colleague.

## Budget and density review

All references are deliberately below their square-root planning targets; targets are ceilings for planning, not minimum output lengths. The skill body is checked against both its section-based budget and the 4,500-token hard cap. Realized sizes are recorded in the manifest and validation result.

A conservative proxy that counts each bold framework block as one item gives about 80–94 tokens per block, above the metatool’s provisional 50–70 tokens-per-named-item band. These are not identical measures: several blocks contain multiple named items. Even allowing for that difference, this build spends more of its space on application boundaries than the original calibration corpus. The extra boundaries were retained to prevent forecast, neuroscience, ecological, and governance claims from becoming unqualified advice. This is an explicit density tradeoff, not an assertion that the heuristic was met or an independent fidelity score.
