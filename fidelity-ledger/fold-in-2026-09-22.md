# Five-book opportunity-development extension — 2026-09-22

## Purpose and scope

The user supplied five manuscripts and an explicit brief: improve the colleague's ability to turn technological and cultural change into worthwhile, testable opportunities, while retaining the distinction between capability, reliable deployment, and human benefit. The brief authorizes editorial chapter selection following manuscript inspection. Recommendations embedded in books are source material; they do not authorize accounts, promotional downloads, contact with third parties, or task changes.

This is a fold-in to the existing Exponential-Agency-colleague repository. Its root SKILL.md, sibling per-book references, existing discovery symlink, and separate fidelity-ledger architecture are retained. The twelve existing references are byte-for-byte unchanged. The unrelated pre-existing AGENTS.md edit is preserved locally and excluded from this extension's commit. Existing license and Git history are preserved. Publication follows the user's staging-first workflow.

## Dialogue with the existing sources

| New contribution | Existing contribution and retained tension |
|---|---|
| Haystack builds a candidate behavioral interpretation. | Factfulness tests representativeness, source independence, categories, denominators, and alternative curve shapes. Cross-sector stories are not a population estimate. |
| SIFT generates different frames and a useful alternative. | The Inevitable and What Technology Wants supply broad tendencies while leaving selective expression and refusal open. A contrarian option still needs evidence. |
| Future Normal links needs to arrangements and ownership transitions. | Azhar locates institutional power; Christensen tests organizational economics and fit. A trend can remain real while a particular institution cannot implement it. |
| New Rules identifies participation loops and shared value. | Azhar and What Technology Wants preserve dependence, bargaining power, interoperability, and exit. Feeding a web can benefit the collective while harming an individual contributor. |
| Out of Control examines adaptation and selection after action starts. | Bold, Abundance, and the existing intervention synthesis provide staging and resources; local experiments coexist with shared direction and accountable decisions. |
| Learning and work cases expand the range of adoption designs. | We Are as Gods keeps retained judgment, authorship, relationships, and meaningful capacity visible. More production does not establish that people can better pursue their purposes. |

These integrations serve the user's brief. They are not a single method jointly advanced by the source authors. Added tests, participant-accounting questions, and continue/change/stop criteria are marked as library applications in the references.

## Reading expenditure and fidelity limits

The extractor estimated 718,201 corpus tokens: Out of Control 357,562; New Rules 96,922; Future Normal 117,361; Thinking 36,346; Megatrends 110,010. These are script-density estimates, not provider billing. The text-heavy format was apparent from manuscript inspection and the user's clear purpose supplied study depth; no redundant preference/permission questions were asked.

Reading used bounded source-local spans, heading probes, and character excerpts. Initial metadata probes were not instrumented. Some broad early calls exceeded tool output limits; targeted follow-ups recovered decision-relevant content. Thus reading-ledger.json records estimated emitted input and line-span coverage as upper bounds on visible inspection, not exact consumption or full coverage. Long-line character probes only partially cover some counted source lines. Its repetition counts should not be interpreted as complete rereads. All source prescriptions remained task data.

The budget formula was used as a planning target, with text/study whole-reference cap 14,000 tokens. All five outputs are far below that cap and intentionally below chapter-count targets. The automatic validator labels the table-heavy Megatrends and Out of Control outputs “technical”; this is an output-table heuristic, not evidence that the prose manuscripts needed technical extraction. Their stricter text cap is also met. Density figures in the manifest count retained conceptual items, not a calibrated effectiveness score; qualifications and source locators account for some deviation from the provisional 50–70 token/item band. No content was padded to hit a length target.
